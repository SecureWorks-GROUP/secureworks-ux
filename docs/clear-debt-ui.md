# Clear Debt view

Ops > Financials > Clear Debt (`#subCleardebt`, host `#clearDebtRoot`) loads `modules/ops-clear-debt-v2.js` and `.css` through `__swOpsStampAssets`. It is Part B of the CFO desk's debt system review (24 Sep 2026), built to the booking screen's standard ([sales-booking-ui.md](sales-booking-ui.md)): same tokens and card grammar, scoped under `#clearDebtRoot`.

**One read.** Opening the tab makes exactly one request: `GET ops-api?action=debt_worklist&timeline=recent` (contract `debt-worklist/v1`, backend `supabase/functions/ops-api/debt_worklist_read_model.ts`). Choosing a debtor, picking an invoice, filtering the timeline, switching channel and typing a draft all render from that payload with no further request. Only the Refresh button reads again. An unknown-action answer shows one line saying the work list is not connected and that this is not a zero balance; any other failure or a different contract version is one red sentence naming it. Nothing is ever shown as an empty book.

**Read only.** The screen has no write, send, note, proposal-mark or provider call. The draft's button is always disabled and says that sending arrives with a later approval step; nothing on screen reads as a recorded approval.

The screen is a work list, phone first:

- **Counts line** from `summary` only, each count with its denominator (`n of 101`) and the read's `as_of` in the header. **Details** holds the denominator sentences, the fact split (present, missing, no job, unknown), the exactly-once reconciliation, what each source read returned, the read's warnings, and what the read does not carry yet (promise dates, a ready-for-Captain verdict, message links, the send route, and fact contents when no debtor's facts were listed).
- **Debtor list** in the read's order. Search matches the whole query inside one field: any contact name spelling, invoice number, reference, linked job number or ambiguous-link candidate. **Show** filters answer only from fields the read carries (overdue, 60+ days, draft waiting, our message was last, not linked, facts missing, stale or unreadable source, contact not confirmed, dispute, blocked, awaiting allocation, not owed, unclassified). "Stale or unreadable source" covers stale Xero, any debtor fault, and a GHL, email, notes or facts source the read marks `stale` or `unreadable`; **Owner** comes from `owners`. Filters never change the counts line. There is no promise-date or ready-for-Captain filter until the read carries those fields.
- **Debtor card**: name, totals, last contact, name variants on the same contact, the standing-alone reason for an unconfirmed contact, badges for identity, job links, Xero freshness, facts and any unreadable source, the debtor's next step naming its invoice, and every debtor fault verbatim.
- **Invoice picker**: every invoice its own radio row, none picked at first. The draft exists only once one invoice is picked, and is about that invoice only.
- **Timeline**: `debtor.timeline.entries` as one stream, newest first, each entry labelled with its provider, kind, direction, time, source (and the other sources that held the same message), invoice or job scope, and "Preview" (or "Preview, cut at 500 characters"). Chips filter that same stream; **Only INV-x** narrows it to the picked invoice. The status line states truncation, the per-job cap, merged copies, each source's state and the stored-copies caveat; email is never called complete because Outlook Sent Items are not captured. An empty filter names the missing source, never "no messages". **Captured facts are timeline entries** with `kind: "fact"` (provider `luna`, source `current_job_context_facts`, a `fact` block with `kind`, `value`, `captured_at`, `source_id` and `state` current or stale); nothing else is treated as a fact, and there is no separate facts panel. The Facts chip counts them only when `sources.facts.timeline_read` is `read`; when it is `unreadable`, `not_read` or absent, one line in the timeline says so instead of a number, and `timeline.facts_cap_reached` names any job whose facts were capped. Per-source health (`status`, `last_success_at`, `stale_after`, `owner`, `recovery_action`) renders under the status line; email's `partial` status reads as inbound copies only.
- **Draft**: Text, Email or Note for the picked invoice. A pending desk proposal of the matching channel loads verbatim with its recipient; the card names every field the read cannot supply (phone, sending line, email address, subject, attachment, mailbox). Edits stay in the browser tab, are marked as unsaved, and can go back to the drafted words.
- **Phone**: the list, or the chosen debtor with **All debtors** to go back.
