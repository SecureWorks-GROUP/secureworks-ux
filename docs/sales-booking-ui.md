# Sales Booking view

Ops > Sales > Performance | Booking (`ops.html#booking`) loads the live `modules/ops-sales-booking.js` and `.css` via `opsWriteModuleAssets`. `#booking` and `#performance` open those tabs; `#sales` and `localStorage.sw_ops_sales_tab` restore the last Sales tab. The calendar defaults to the current Perth week and the signed-in scoper UUID; unmatched accounts choose explicitly. Previous-week navigation cannot enter a past week.

The screen is a dispatcher's work list, phone first: one line of counts (to contact, waiting on a reply, booked per day), a **Details** fold for what the read could not see, sources, visit history and the read-only GHL pipeline, a **To contact** list ordered loudest customer first (an unanswered inbound message ranks first, oldest unanswered at the top) with each person's last words quoted, one card for the chosen person, and the day as a column. The to-contact count is that same week-wide To contact set; search filters the list, not the totals. A failed read is one red sentence naming what could not be read. There is a Refresh button; search repaints only the list so the box keeps focus, and typing in the text box repaints only its footer.

The card shows the last three GHL messages verbatim, the proposed text (editable), the sending line and the customer's phone ending, and, when a day is proposed, the arrival window in words, the customer's quoted evidence, the checks, and the calendars **Book it** writes (the read's `calendar_write.preview.targets` when present, else the named GHL calendar plus Marnin's Outlook for Stratco). A clash names the other booking and its calendar beside the text and on the proposed visit. Slot gates (calendar unread, expiry, hours, the protected band, a clash) disable **Book it** only; **Send this text** stays enabled so the owner can offer another time. Booked day-column entries carry a GHL or Outlook badge from the event's `source` (anything not naming Outlook is GHL); proposed slots are dashed and unbadged. Overlapping entries share the column, and the rows grow so their names and times stay visible.

**Send this text** and **Book it** each do three things on one press: record the owner's approval of the exact content on screen through `sales_booking_approval_write`, call `sales_booking_send` or `sales_booking_book` with `{approval_id}` (the approval's `id`, else its `binding_hash`), then state the result in words (sent, booked, trial run, refused with the reason, or unclear). A booked result names the write's `written` calendars when present, else the same Book it targets. The text box locks, draft focus is not restored, and **Use the proposed text** is hidden and ignored, while an approval or send/book is in flight, so the words on screen cannot change until the result is shown; if the snapshot then no longer matches the words that were approved, the executor call is aborted. An edited text is approved as the edited words: the snapshot carries `variant: "edited"` and a `content_hash` computed exactly as ops-api `bookingContentHash` does (key-sorted canonical JSON, SHA-256), so the approval covers nothing else. Typing the proposed words back returns to the producer's own template and hash. When an action does not exist yet (unknown action), the card says sending or booking is not connected yet and that nothing was sent or booked; it never fakes success. An unclear answer blocks another press until a fresh read. Only Marnin's Stratco profile can approve here, and identity stays the GHL contact id. Uncertain proposals say Needs a person and give the reason. GHL calendar read errors never paint free capacity. Diary occupancy and booked-visit matching use exact GHL `contact_id`, `opportunity_id` or `event_id` (or a `Scope:` title); name and suburb never identify a visit.

Booked visits support Happened or Did not happen, with one of three reasons for the latter. Quote owed defaults on for Happened and can be unticked. Until `booking_flow.visit_outcomes_read` is `complete`, the visit-outcomes block inside Details is only the calm line "Visit outcomes are not connected yet". When that read is complete, the last seven days' visits without an outcome appear under the list as visits to close out, and other booked visits stay in the Details history fold. Corrections append a superseding record; recording sends no message.
