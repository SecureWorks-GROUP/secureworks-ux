# Sales Booking view

Ops > Sales > Performance | Booking (`ops.html#booking`) loads the live `modules/ops-sales-booking.js` and `.css` via `opsWriteModuleAssets`. `#booking` and `#performance` open those tabs; `#sales` and `localStorage.sw_ops_sales_tab` restore the last Sales tab. The calendar defaults to the current Perth week and the signed-in scoper UUID; unmatched accounts choose explicitly. Previous-week navigation cannot enter a past week.

The screen is **Build the week**, a three-pane layout: the people to book on the left, the **Monday to Friday week in the middle**, and the chosen person's conversation, text and visit on the right. Above them sit one line of counts (to contact, waiting on a reply, booked per day) and a **Details** fold for what the read could not see, sources, visit history and the read-only GHL pipeline. The list is a **To contact** list ordered loudest customer first (an unanswered inbound message ranks first, oldest unanswered at the top) with each person's last words quoted. The week paints every calendar entry the read returns once, on its day (`renderWeek`; each day is a CSS subgrid on the week's shared 15-minute rows, so hours stay level across days while a row grows to fit words); a day with overlapping entries gets more width and an empty non-working day less, so side-by-side entries keep whole words. Each lead's proposed visit is exactly one dashed orange card on its day (a small **Proposed** chip, the name, the suburb, then the time); a lead with no proposed time has no card. Choosing a lead on the left lights its card up. A proposed visit that runs into a calendar entry or another customer's held time turns red and names that booking on the card, e.g. "Clashes with Scope: Melanie N, Piara Waters at 10:45am". **Booked with another scoper** is the server's word only: when a case carries `scope_appointment` (`{start_iso, end_iso?, owner_name?, owner_resource_id?, status?}`, added by the backend lead-owner rule for a live scope visit in any scoper's GHL calendar), the lead leaves To contact for Booked, its row and card say e.g. "Booked with Khairo, Tue 29 Sep 10:00am", and no case with a live `scope_appointment` paints its proposal on the week (the real visit is already in the diary). When `owner_resource_id` is someone else, the right pane shows "Booked with Khairo, Tue 29 Sep 10:00am" in place of a proposed visit and both **Book it** and **Send this text** are disabled: no new time may be offered or booked. A cancelled or no-show status is not a booking. **Tap a free time to pick the visit.** When the read carries a lead's own free times (`case.free_times.days[].arrival_windows[]`, backend `docs/sales-booking-live-availability.md`: 60-minute arrival windows every five minutes, `end_iso` 30 minutes on site after the latest arrival), the chosen lead's free times are green bands on the week (one band per run of windows five minutes apart). A tap picks the server's window nearest the tapped point, exactly as sent (`from_iso`, `to_iso`, `end_iso`; the screen never works a time out), opens Pick a visit on it, and rewrites the text's day and time to match (e.g. "on Friday 2 October, arriving between 12:15 and 1:15pm"), signed with the business name of the line it goes from. Changing the pick from the Pick a visit day, window or start lists rewrites the text the same way. Words the owner typed are never overwritten: the card offers "Rewrite it for ..." instead, and **Send this text** stays disabled until the text names the picked day and time. A pick left unfinished drops text the screen wrote for the earlier pick back to the proposed text; if the owner edited that text, **Send this text** stays disabled with a plain reason until the pick is finished. The pick then goes through the same server check as any picked visit before it can be approved; a refusal is one plain sentence and nothing is booked. Bands sit over the chosen lead's own proposal and under other leads' proposals. A lead booked with someone else has no bands. Without the field nothing is inferred from names, stages or threads. At tablet widths (900–1239px), the list and conversation sit side by side with the week below both. On a phone (below 900px), selecting a person hides the list and shows **All leads**, the day strip, that one day, then the conversation; **All leads** returns to the list, with the selected day below it. The to-contact count is that same week-wide To contact set; search filters the list, not the totals. A failed read is one red sentence naming what could not be read. There is a Refresh button; search repaints only the list so the box keeps focus, and typing in the text box repaints only its footer.

The card shows the last three GHL messages verbatim, the proposed text (editable), the sending line and the customer's phone ending, and, when a day is proposed, the arrival window in words, the customer's quoted evidence, the checks, and the calendars **Book it** writes (the read's `calendar_write.preview.targets` when present, else the named GHL calendar plus Marnin's Outlook for Stratco). A clash names the other booking and its calendar beside the text and on the proposed visit. Slot gates (calendar unread, expiry, hours, the protected band, a clash) disable **Book it** only; **Send this text** stays enabled so the owner can offer another time, except for a lead booked with another scoper, where both are disabled. Booked day-column entries carry a GHL or Outlook badge from the event's `source` (anything not naming Outlook is GHL); proposed slots are dashed and unbadged. Overlapping entries share the column, and the rows grow so their names and times stay visible.

**Send this text** and **Book it** each do three things on one press: record the owner's approval of the exact content on screen through `sales_booking_approval_write`, call `sales_booking_send` or `sales_booking_book` with `{approval_id}` (the approval's `id`, else its `binding_hash`), then state the result in words (sent, booked, trial run, refused with the reason, or unclear). A booked result names the write's `written` calendars when present, else the same Book it targets. The text box locks, draft focus is not restored, and **Use the proposed text** is hidden and ignored, while an approval or send/book is in flight, so the words on screen cannot change until the result is shown; if the snapshot then no longer matches the words that were approved, the executor call is aborted. On a read without the owner's path, an edited text is approved as the edited words: the snapshot carries `variant: "edited"` and a `content_hash` computed exactly as ops-api `bookingContentHash` does (key-sorted canonical JSON, SHA-256), so the approval covers nothing else; with the owner's path an edit goes through the check below instead. Typing the proposed words back returns to the producer's own template and hash. When an action does not exist yet (unknown action), the card says sending or booking is not connected yet and that nothing was sent or booked; it never fakes success. An unclear answer blocks another press until a fresh read. Only Marnin's Stratco profile can approve here, and identity stays the GHL contact id. Uncertain proposals say Needs a person and give the reason. GHL calendar read errors never paint free capacity. Diary occupancy and booked-visit matching use exact GHL `contact_id`, `opportunity_id` or `event_id` (or a `Scope:` title); name and suburb never identify a visit.

**The owner's own text or visit** (`owner-authored-v1`, backend PR https://github.com/SecureWorks-GROUP/secureworks-backend/pull/900, contract section "Owner-authored approvals" in backend `docs/sales-booking-confirmation-api.md`). When the read advertises `booking_flow.owner_approval_write: "owner-authored-v1"` and the case carries an eligible `owner_booking`, the owner can write or edit the text on any lead's card, and every lead can pick its own time in a small **Pick a visit** picker (day, arrive from, arrival window) built only from the read's rulebook (`bookable_dates`, hours, window lengths, visit length, protected band with travel either side). A lead with a proposed time shows that proposal first, exactly as before (one-press **Book it**, quotes, checks), with a **Pick a different time** link that opens the picker and a **Use the proposed time** link back; a lead with no proposed time opens on the picker. An unedited engine text still takes the engine path above until a time is picked; Book it on a proposed time stays that one-press engine path until a different time is picked. On the owner's path each step is two presses: **Send this text** or **Book it** asks the server to check (`owner_input` with `dry_run`), and the card shows what the server built, exactly (the text and the line and phone it goes between, or the visit, title and address and the calendars it goes in), with the checks in words; **Approve and send** or **Approve and book** then records the approval with that check's `prepared_at` and `content_hash`, calls `sales_booking_send` or `sales_booking_book` with the returned `approval_id`, and states the result. **Change it** drops the check. While a check is open or anything is in flight, the text box and the picker are locked; a change after the check, or a check older than 15 minutes, is never approved. A text sent while a time is picked always carries that time as its `offer` so the server holds it against other leads. That lead's own offer never blocks its own Book it. A successful book or offer is added to the day's occupancy so the column and the other cards' clash gates agree with GHL and Outlook without Refresh, then the list is read again quietly (no spinner, no locked buttons); what the screen just did stays on the day through that and later reads, for up to 30 minutes, until the read carries it, so the slot never reads as free. A named refusal is one plain sentence beside the button, naming the clashing entry when the server does. When the read does not advertise the owner's path, the screen behaves exactly as before; when the server answers the owner's path as an unknown action, the card says it is not connected yet and nothing was sent or booked.

Booked visits support Happened or Did not happen, with one of three reasons for the latter. Quote owed defaults on for Happened and can be unticked. Until `booking_flow.visit_outcomes_read` is `complete`, the visit-outcomes block inside Details is only the calm line "Visit outcomes are not connected yet". When that read is complete, the last seven days' visits without an outcome appear under the list as visits to close out, and other booked visits stay in the Details history fold. Corrections append a superseding record; recording sends no message.
