Getting started with the trade app

This is how you see your jobs and get paid. Watch the video, then follow the steps.

About 90 seconds. No sound: the words are on screen. The pay rate shown is made up.

The steps

  1. Open your invite email and tap the link. It opens the app and signs you in.

  2. Set a password. If the app asks for one, choose it now. If it does not, go to Profile, then Password.

    Locked out later? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

  3. Add the app to your home screen. iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the menu (three dots), then Add to Home screen.

  4. Fill in My Details. Go to Profile, then My Details. Enter your ABN, BSB and account number, pick your GST status and tap Save Details.

    Check it says Saved to your account. No details, no pay.

  5. Check your pay rate. Profile shows My Hourly Rate. If it is blank or says Not set yet, tell the office.

  6. Enter your times for each job. Jobs you are booked on show up on that week's invoice.

    Go to Pay, then Weekly Invoice. On each job, enter the date and your start and finish times.

    On a patio job, open the job, find Group work order, tap Join and save your hours there.

  7. Send one invoice a week. At the end of the week go to Pay, then Weekly Invoice, and tap Submit Invoice. No invoice means no pay for that week.

Do not type your pay into the amount box at the bottom of the invoice. That box takes money off your invoice. It is for things like a loan repayment. Your pay comes from the times you enter on each job.
